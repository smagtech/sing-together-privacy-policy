# Privacy Policy for Sing Together

**Effective Date:** October 29, 2026  
**Developer:** MAGTECH  
**App:** Sing Together (`com.magtech.SingTogether`)  

Sing Together is designed with privacy as a foundational principle. We respect your privacy and do not collect, rent, or sell your personal identifiable information or audio recordings.

---

### 1. Permissions and Data Handling
- **Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`):** Used strictly to capture real-time vocals for chorus singing, audio monitoring, and local session recordings. Audio is processed on-device and streamed locally via peer-to-peer Wi-Fi; it is never uploaded to any cloud server.
- **Camera (`CAMERA`):** Used only to scan the Host Singer's QR code or capture lyrics text via on-device ML Kit OCR. Images are never stored remotely or transmitted off-device.
- **Storage / Music & Audio (`READ_MEDIA_AUDIO`, `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`):** Used exclusively when backing up or restoring your lyrics and audio recordings to your device's local storage.
- **Nearby Devices & Local Network:** Used for discovering and pairing Chorus/Singer devices over Wi-Fi Direct or local hotspot without requiring an internet connection.

---

### 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The application integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show rewarded advertisements:
- Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, advertising IDs, IP addresses, and app analytics for ad serving and fraud prevention in compliance with [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

---

### 3. Data Storage and Security
All user data—including custom lyrics, recorded audio files, and user preferences—remains stored locally on your device. You have full control over creating, exporting, and deleting your data.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy
Sing Together does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
